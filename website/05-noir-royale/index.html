The Sculptor

Three decades of ironwork, now available as a placement program.

Ikeuchi trained as a welder in his family's ironworks on Shikoku before turning to sculpture in his late forties. Since 2009 his work has been collected and awarded across Japan's most serious exhibitions, and every piece is still hand-forged in his own workshop in Kochi.

This program packages that craft for organizations that need to place a single, defensible object — vetted, insurable, and proven able to stand outdoors for years — rather than commission original art from scratch.

Placement Program

Built for four categories of client.

Residential Developers
A signature object for the lobby or entrance of a residential tower or condominium — presented to prospective buyers as part of the building's identity, not an add-on.
Private Members' Clubs
A single sculptural object for a lounge, bar, or reception — chosen to be noticed, discussed, and remembered by members and their guests.
Hospitality Groups
Weatherproof enough for an entrance or courtyard, refined enough for an interior lobby or bath-house approach, across one property or a portfolio.
Private Estates
A piece scaled and sited for a private garden or estate, grown alongside the landscape rather than placed against it.

Process

A defined path from proposal to installation.

01

Consultation

We review your site — light, sightlines, scale, and what it should say.

02

Previsualization

The piece is placed in a cinematic render of its future setting for sign-off.

03

Forging

Hand-built in Ikeuchi's Kochi workshop, to your approved scale and finish.

04

Delivery & Care

Shipped, installed, and weather-tested, with an ongoing maintenance plan.
