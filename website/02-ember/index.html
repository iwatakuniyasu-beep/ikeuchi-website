The Sculptor

From the family furnace to the national stage.

Ikeuchi trained as a welder inside his family's ironworks on Shikoku, twice placing in Kochi Prefecture's welding competitions before he ever exhibited as an artist. In his late forties he redirected three decades of technique toward sculpture, and has exhibited every year since 2009.

Each work is still hand-forged in his own Kochi workshop — the same hands, the same fire, the same iron he has worked since he was young.

Process

From first sketch to standing in your entrance.

Consultation

We review your site — light, sightlines, scale, and what the space is meant to say.

Previsualization

The piece is placed in a cinematic render of its future setting, so scale and mood are approved before forging begins.

Forging

Hand-built in Ikeuchi's own workshop in Kochi, using welding techniques practiced since his youth.

Installation & Care

Delivered, installed, and weather-tested, with an ongoing maintenance plan for outdoor and coastal sites.
