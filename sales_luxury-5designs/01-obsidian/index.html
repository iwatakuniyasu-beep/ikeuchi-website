The Sculptor

Thirty years at the forge, before the first gallery wall.

Ikeuchi trained as a welder in his family's ironworks on the island of Shikoku, twice placing in Kochi Prefecture's welding competitions before he ever called himself a sculptor. In his late forties he turned that trade toward fine art — and since 2009 his work has been collected and awarded across Japan's most serious sculpture exhibitions.

Every piece shown here is still hand-forged in his own workshop in Kochi, using the iron techniques he has practiced since youth. Nothing is outsourced, and nothing is cast from a mould twice.

Process

From consultation to installation.

Consultation

We review the site — light, sightlines, scale, and what the space is meant to say.

Previsualization

The piece is placed in a cinematic render of its future setting, so scale and mood can be approved before forging begins.

Forging

Hand-built in Ikeuchi's own workshop in Kochi, using welding techniques practiced since his youth.

Installation & Care

Delivered, installed, and weather-tested, with a maintenance plan for outdoor and coastal placements.
